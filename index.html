  1. 2023

    Porto, Portugal

    Speaker

    “Customizing Utility Pages: Addressing the Business Need of a Seamless User Experience”

    Building a great site is not only about making pages easier to create, but also about simplifying how people move through them. System pages such as 404, login and search pages shape the end-user experience even though they sit outside the main navigation, and adapting them to each client’s needs has traditionally taken significant effort.

    This session showed how marketers and non-technical users can customize these native portal pages with the page editor, making it easier to align them with brand design guidelines.

    • Page management
    • User experience
    • Customization

  2. 2024

    Budapest, Hungary

    Speaker

    “Transforming Content Creation with AI”

    An exploration of how different AI models can optimize and streamline content creation within Liferay. The session introduced an advanced AI assistant that uses multiple models to generate different types of content, from blogs and categories to complete page layouts, all within a unified platform.

    It covered content generation, translation and categorization for specific audiences and markets, and demonstrated how images can be turned into dynamic page layouts, reducing design time while keeping consistency and quality.

    • Artificial intelligence
    • Content creation
    • Digital experience

  4. 2026

    Fourth DEVCON

    Speaker · Product Owner

    “Personalize Everything: Client-Side Audiences and Personalisation in Liferay”

    This session explores Liferay DXP’s new client-side personalisation framework. It shows how to define audiences, attach variations to page elements and deliver fast, flexible experiences directly in the browser. Combined with Liferay Data Platform segments, audiences can also adapt experiences using real-time user behaviour and external data, such as CRM account information.

    A personal milestone

    This session is closely connected to one of my first major projects as a Product Owner at Liferay, bringing together customer needs, technical capabilities and the delivery of personalised digital experiences.
